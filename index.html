Who We Are

Building a future filled with possibility.

Good News for Orphans is a registered organization working to support vulnerable children and strengthen the community around them.

Our work is rooted in compassion, dignity, responsibility and the belief that every child should have an equal path to success.

  • Education and learning support
  • Food and basic needs
  • Child wellbeing and protection
  • Community support
  • Opportunities for a better future
Our Work

Where Your Support Can Help

Our programmes are designed around practical needs and the long-term wellbeing of vulnerable children.

Education

Supporting children's access to education, learning materials and opportunities to develop their potential.

Food & Basic Needs

Helping address essential needs such as food, clothing and other basic necessities.

Community & Wellbeing

Working with the community to promote children's wellbeing, dignity and opportunity.
