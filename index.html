Children First

Our Child Safeguarding Commitment

Protecting children's dignity and privacy

Children deserve to be protected from abuse, exploitation, unnecessary exposure, and inappropriate use of their personal information.

We do not use children simply as a way to attract donations. Any photographs, stories, or other information involving children should be published only when the appropriate consent and safeguarding requirements have been met.

We avoid publishing children's full names, precise locations, school details, personal contact information, or sensitive family circumstances where doing so could put a child at risk.

If you have a safeguarding concern, please contact the organization directly rather than posting details about a child publicly.
